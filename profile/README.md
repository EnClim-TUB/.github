# EnClim | Energy Engineering and Climate Protection

**Technische Universität Berlin | Institute of Energy Engineering**

Welcome to the GitHub organization of **EnClim**, the research group for Energy Engineering and Climate Protection at TU Berlin, led by Prof. Dr. Fontina Petrakopoulou.

Our research focuses on the development, modelling, optimization, and assessment of sustainable energy systems, with particular emphasis on:

- Energy system modelling and optimization
- Thermodynamic and exergy analysis
- Energy conversion and storage technologies
- Renewable energy integration and sector coupling
- Advanced control and operation of energy systems
- Techno-economic and environmental assessment

## Open-source software

### [ExerPy – Exergy Analysis in Python](https://github.com/oemof/exerpy)

ExerPy is an open-source Python tool for exergy analysis of energy conversion systems. It enables the evaluation of exergy flows, exergy destruction, and thermodynamic inefficiencies using simulation data.

Developed by researchers at TU Berlin and hosted within the [oemof](https://github.com/oemof) open-source community.
