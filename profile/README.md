# EnClim | Energy Engineering and Climate Protection

**Technische Universität Berlin | Institute of Energy Engineering**

Welcome to the GitHub organization of **EnClim**, the research group for Energy Engineering and Climate Protection at TU Berlin, led by **Prof. Dr.-Ing. Fontina Petrakopoulou**.

We develop and apply advanced modelling, simulation, optimization, and assessment methods to address challenges in sustainable energy systems. Our research spans innovative energy-conversion technologies, integrated and resilient energy systems, and the interactions between energy, water, and climate.

**Our approach goes beyond conventional efficiency and cost considerations**, incorporating environmental impacts, resource use, resilience, and socioeconomic aspects to support the development of sustainable and climate-neutral energy systems.

Through open-source software development, we aim to make advanced scientific methods more accessible, reliable, and reproducible.

## Open-source software

### [ExerPy – Exergy Analysis in Python](https://github.com/oemof/exerpy)

ExerPy is an open-source Python framework that **automates exergy analysis and integrates it with energy-system simulation software**.

By connecting with tools such as TESPy, Ebsilon Professional, and Aspen Plus, ExerPy streamlines data processing and exergy calculations, reducing manual effort and the risk of user errors. Its standardized workflows enable consistent, reproducible thermodynamic assessments across different simulation platforms.

Developed by researchers at TU Berlin and hosted within the [oemof](https://github.com/oemof) open-source community.
